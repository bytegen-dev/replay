Replay has two pages and a public API.

<Cards>
  <Card title="Hindsight" href="/docs/hindsight">
    See what a buy from the past would be worth today: once or on a schedule, with or without leverage.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Wayback" href="/docs/wayback">
    The price of a token or stock at any past date and time, typed the way people say it.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Public API" href="/docs/api">
    The same calculations as JSON, for apps and bots.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Agent skill" href="/docs/agent-skill">
    Teach an AI agent to answer "what would a monthly buy be worth?" with real prices.
  </Card>
</Cards>

## Start here

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Pick what to check
    Open [Hindsight](/) and choose **Stocks**, **Crypto** or **Currencies**.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Say how much and when
    An amount, how often you buy, and a start and end month.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### See what it is worth
    The result is a sentence, a chart you can replay as a video, and a table of every buy you can export.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warn" title="A backtest, not advice">
  Replay looks at the past. It is not a forecast and not financial advice, and it ignores taxes, fees and spreads.
</Callout>
