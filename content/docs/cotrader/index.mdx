The CoTrader watches a Solana wallet, either the one you trade with or one you wish to track. It follows each position against rules you set yourself, and tells you when one is met. **It tells you. It never trades for you.**

## What works today

| | |
|---|---|
| **Wallets** | Up to three wallets, named, searchable, sortable and hideable; portfolio (SOL, USDC and tokens) and P&L over 24h, 7d or 30d |
| **Trades** | Open positions and history, with entry price, market cap and links to trade sites |
| **Your own rules** | A stop alert, a give-back alert, a daily profit target, a daily loss limit and a trim plan |
| **Alerts** | On the Alerts screen, and as notifications on the device where you turn them on |
| **Call scorecard** | Upload a Telegram chat export and see how the calls in it turned out |
| **Search** | Cmd+K (Ctrl+K) finds any screen, setting, rule, your wallets and tokens, and any token |
| **Token pages** | Price, DexScreener's chart, market cap, liquidity, volume and where it trades |
| **Settings** | Light, dark or system theme, a main and a second currency, hide balances, pop-ups and sound |

## Coming soon

Trading from a token page, alerts when a token's biggest holders start selling, text commands, a Telegram bot, an optional auto mode through Merrymen, connections to AI agents, personal API keys and webhooks. None of these are on yet, and nothing in the app pretends they are.

<Callout type="warn" title="Not financial advice">
  The CoTrader reports what your own rules say about positions that already exist. It is not a recommendation to buy or sell anything. Prices come from public sources and can be wrong or late, and past results say nothing about the next trade.
</Callout>
