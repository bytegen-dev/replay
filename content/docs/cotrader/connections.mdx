None of these are available yet. The Connections screen lists them so you can see what is planned.

## AI agents

- **Merrymen:** an optional auto mode, where an agent trades for you inside limits you sign.
- **Claude, Grokbot and Hermes:** ask an agent about your wallets, positions and alerts.
- **Your own agent:** connect any agentic setup of your own over an API.

## Rooms

Planned, and not available yet.

## For your own tools

- **Personal API keys:** let your own scripts read your wallets, positions and alerts.
- **Webhooks:** send an alert to a URL of yours the moment one of your rules is met.

Replay's [public API](/docs/api) and [agent skill](/docs/agent-skill) already exist for its backtesting tools. They are separate from this.
