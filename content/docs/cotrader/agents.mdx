A CoTrader is the assistant you ask about your wallets. You can make up to **five**, each with its own name, picture and way of speaking. They read your wallets, positions, trades and alerts and answer in plain words. **They only read and explain. They never trade.**

## Making one

On the **Agents** screen, choose **Create a CoTrader**:

- **Picture:** a generated avatar (use **Randomize** for another), or upload your own. An uploaded picture is cut square and shrunk before it is saved.
- **Name:** up to 24 characters. Two of your CoTraders cannot share a name, ignoring capitals and spaces. A new one starts as "CoTrader", then "CoTrader 2" and so on.
- **How it speaks:** calm, direct or encouraging.
- **Notes (optional):** how you like to be spoken to. They change its style only, never its rules.

The newest one you make becomes your **default**, until you choose another in its options. The floating button in the corner always opens the default.

## Sleep mode

Turn on sleep mode in a CoTrader's options to stop it answering without deleting it. It keeps its name, picture and notes. If the default goes to sleep, another takes its place and you are told which.

## Chatting

Choose the chat icon next to a CoTrader, or the floating button. Ask about your wallets, positions, trades and alerts. Answers type in, with bold text and lists where they help.

- **Reply to a message** to point at it, **copy** a message, and jump to the **latest** message after scrolling up.
- **Expand** opens the chat as its own page, inside the app.
- On a wide screen the chat is a window you can **drag** (by the grip at the top), **resize** and **pin**. A pinned chat stays on the screen while you use the app, with no dimming of the page. You can pin up to five, one for each CoTrader. Positions and pins are remembered in your browser, and a different screen size moves a window sensibly.
- In **Settings** you can have pinned chats fade while you are not using them, and choose how much.
- The search (Cmd+K or Ctrl+K) finds your CoTraders and the messages in your chats.

## Who answers

Chat needs a model behind it. Either:

- **The shared model:** free, with **10 messages a day** across your CoTraders, as a way to try it. It is shared by many people and is sometimes busy. Trying again is the fix. When the 10 are used up, connect your own account to keep chatting, or come back the next day.
- **Your own account:** connect your OpenRouter account, or any provider that speaks the same language as OpenAI. There is no daily limit from us, and your provider bills you. Your key is stored sealed and only used for your chats. You can change or disconnect it on the Agents screen.

## What is kept

Each conversation is saved on our servers, the newest 200 messages for each CoTrader, so it is still there after a reload and on another device. Failed answers are not saved. **Clear the conversation** in the chat to remove it, or delete the CoTrader and its conversations go with it.

To answer, your messages, the CoTrader's name and notes, your display name, and what it looks up (your watched wallets and their names, positions, trades and alerts) are sent to the model provider. See [Is this safe?](/docs/cotrader/safety) and the [privacy page](/privacy).
