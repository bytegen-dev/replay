title: Trades

The Trades screen has two tabs, and a row of wallet buttons above them to look at one wallet or all of them.

## Open positions

What your wallets hold now, one row per token:

- **Token:** its picture, ticker and name. Tap it to open its [token page](/docs/cotrader/tokens). A token the data sources have never heard of shows its shortened address.
- **Avg entry:** what you paid per token, in dollars, with the market cap it had at that price. It is worked out from the SOL you spent at today's SOL price, so for an old position it is approximate. A token received free says so.
- **Now:** the current price and market cap.
- **Held, 24h and Liquidity**, then the **value** (with what it cost) and the **P&L** in percent, and when the position opened.
- **Trade:** small logos that open the token on Axiom, GMGN and Pump.fun.

A token with no live price shows **Dead**.

## History

Every buy, sell and free receive, newest first: the token, what it was, the price and market cap it happened at, the amount, and for a sell what it made or lost over what those tokens cost.

## Large lists

Fifty rows are shown at a time. **Load more** adds fifty. On a phone the tables become compact rows so nothing has to scroll sideways, and on a wide screen a table that is wider than the screen can be dragged sideways with the mouse.
