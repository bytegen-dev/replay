## Add a wallet

Choose **Add a wallet** on the Wallets screen, or on the Overview if you have none yet. You can add up to three. There are three ways:

1. **Connect your wallet and sign.** Your wallet extension signs a short message that proves the wallet is yours. Nothing is sent and it costs nothing.
2. **Watch the wallet you signed in with**, when you signed in with a wallet extension. Signing in already proved it.
3. **Watch without signing.** Paste an address. No extension needed, and you get the same alerts. This is how you track a wallet that is not yours.

<Callout title="Signing is optional">
  Everything the CoTrader reads is public on the blockchain, so watching never needs a signature. Signing only shows the wallet is yours.
</Callout>

## Your wallets

Each wallet is a card. With more than one, the cards stack and can be opened and closed independently, so you can have several open at once.

- **Name a wallet** with the pencil beside its address. The name shows everywhere the wallet does. Clear it to go back to the address.
- **Search** your wallets by name or address, and use **Sort and filter** (the sliders button) to order them by what they are worth, by their latest trade, or by when you added them, or to show only wallets that hold tokens, are up or down today, have no rules on, or are still being read. These choices are kept in your browser.
- **Hide** a wallet to take it out of the list. It is still watched and still alerts you. Turn on **Show hidden** in the filters to see it again.
- **Trades** opens that wallet's positions and history. **Recap** opens [Replay's recap](https://replay.bytegen.dev/recap) for the wallet in a new tab.
- The **bin** button stops watching a wallet after you confirm. Its positions, your rules for it and the alerts it raised are removed. Nothing in the wallet itself changes.

## Portfolio and P&L

- **Portfolio** is what the wallet holds right now: its SOL, its USDC and its tokens, valued at current prices.
- **P&L** is switchable between **24h**, **7d** and **30d**. It is the profit realized by sells in that window, plus the gain so far on positions opened in it. A sell only counts for the tokens whose purchase the CoTrader can see, so tokens bought before its history starts, or received free, do not show their whole sale as profit. Treat it as a guide, not an exact account.
- Both follow your [currency settings](/docs/cotrader/settings): a main currency, and an optional second one as a small line underneath.

## What it reads

- The wallet's transaction history, from [Helius](https://www.helius.dev), and its SOL and USDC balances.
- The **first sync is silent.** It learns what the wallet already holds, so adding a wallet never floods you with alerts about old holdings.
- Later syncs read only what is new. Positions are rebuilt from the wallet's trades, first in, first out. A token that was **received free** (an airdrop) counts at zero cost, so a percentage gain is not worked out for it.
- Dust is left out: a token you **bought** for less than 0.002 SOL, and a free token worth less than 0.02 SOL, are not watched.
- Prices come from [DexScreener](https://dexscreener.com), 30 tokens at a time, from each token's deepest pool. A token with no pool that holds real liquidity has no live price. It shows as **Dead** and is not run against your rules.

## Your rules

Open **Rules** on a wallet's card. Each rule has a switch. Switching one on gives it a starting number you can change. A rule that is on needs a number above zero.

| Rule | It tells you when |
|---|---|
| **Stop alert** | A token is down this much from what you put in |
| **Give-back alert** | A token falls this far from its best profit |
| **Daily profit target** | Today's result reaches this |
| **Daily loss limit** | Today's loss reaches this |
| **Trim plan** | A token reaches a gain you chose, and how much of it you planned to sell. Up to three levels |

A rule tells you **once** for each time you hold a token. Sell a token and buy it again later and it has its own alerts. **The CoTrader never sells for you.** It only tells you.

## Alerts

Every alert lands on the **Alerts** screen, newest first and grouped by day. Each has an icon and a colour for its kind: a new position, a stop, a trim level, a give-back, a daily target, a daily loss.

To get an alert when the app is closed, turn on notifications on the Alerts screen or in [Settings](/docs/cotrader/settings). On an iPhone, add the app to your Home Screen first.

## How syncing works

Your wallets are synced by the server, so alerts do not depend on the app being open: a scheduler checks every wallet about once a minute, and a webhook from Helius syncs a wallet within seconds of a new transaction. Opening the Wallets screen syncs once, and the **Sync** button syncs on demand. The screens otherwise just read what the server has stored.

Everything read about a wallet is shared for 20 seconds, whoever is watching it. Two accounts watching the same wallet see the same figures, and syncing again inside that window costs nothing and leaves the numbers as they were.
