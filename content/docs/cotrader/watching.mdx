## Add a wallet

Choose **Add a wallet** on the Watch screen, or on the Overview if you have none yet. You can add up to three. There are three ways:

1. **Connect your wallet and sign.** Your wallet extension signs a short message that proves the wallet is yours. Nothing is sent and it costs nothing.
2. **Watch the wallet you signed in with**, when you signed in with a wallet extension. Signing in already proved it.
3. **Watch without signing.** Paste an address. No extension needed, and you get the same alerts. This is how you track a wallet that is not yours.

<Callout title="Signing is optional">
  Everything the CoTrader reads is public on the blockchain, so watching never needs a signature. Signing only shows the wallet is yours.
</Callout>

## What it reads

- The wallet's transaction history, from [Helius](https://www.helius.dev), and its SOL balance at each check.
- The **first check is silent.** It learns what the wallet already holds, so adding a wallet never floods you with alerts about old holdings.
- Later checks read only what is new. A new trade shows up as "Watching ..." and your rules speak when they are met.
- Positions are rebuilt from the wallet's trades, first in, first out. A coin that was **received free** (an airdrop) counts at zero cost, so a percentage gain is not worked out for it.
- Dust is left out: a coin you **bought** for less than 0.002 SOL, and a free coin worth less than 0.02 SOL, are not watched.
- A price comes from public price sources. A position with no recent price is shown without a value and is not run against your rules.

## Your rules

Each rule has a switch. Switching one on gives it a starting number you can change. A rule that is on needs a number above zero.

| Rule | It tells you when |
|---|---|
| **Stop alert** | A coin is down this much from what you put in |
| **Give-back alert** | A coin falls this far from its best profit |
| **Daily profit target** | Today's result reaches this |
| **Daily loss limit** | Today's loss reaches this |
| **Trim plan** | A coin reaches a gain you chose, and how much of it you planned to sell. Up to three levels |

A rule tells you **once** for each time you hold a coin. Sell a coin and buy it again later and it has its own alerts. **The CoTrader never sells for you.** It only tells you.

## Alerts

Every alert lands on the **Alerts** screen, newest first and grouped by day. Each has an icon and a colour for its kind: a new position, a stop, a trim level, a give-back, a daily target, a daily loss.

To get an alert when the app is closed, turn on notifications on the Alerts screen or in [Settings](/docs/cotrader/settings). On an iPhone, add the app to your Home Screen first.

## How often it checks

While the app is open it checks every minute. The server also checks your wallets in the background, so alerts do not depend on the app being open.
