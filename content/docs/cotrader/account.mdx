The **Account** screen is yours: who is signed in, how, and your plan.

## Your name and picture

Choose **Edit profile** to set:

- **Picture:** randomize one, or upload your own. Until you choose one, you have the blocky picture made from your wallet.
- **Display name:** up to 24 characters. Leave it empty to show your wallet address instead.

Your name and picture show on the Account screen and at the foot of the sidebar. **Your CoTraders call you by your display name** when it is natural to, so they know who they are talking to. It is only a name: a CoTrader never takes an instruction from it.

## How you signed in

The screen shows whether you signed in with a wallet, email, Google or X, your plan, and the wallet address your account is tied to. Sign out from the same screen.

To have an account and everything on it deleted, see the [privacy page](/privacy).
