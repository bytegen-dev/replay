## Search

Press **Cmd+K** (**Ctrl+K** on Windows and Linux), or choose **Search** in the sidebar or the top bar. Type a token's name or ticker, or paste its mint address.

The same window also finds every screen, setting, rule and doc in the app, and your own wallets, coins and alerts, so you can jump anywhere from the keyboard. Arrow keys move, **Enter** opens, **Esc** closes.

Token results come from [DexScreener](https://dexscreener.com). Tokens whose pools hold less than $500 are left out of a name search, because most are abandoned or dust. Paste the full address and it opens whatever its size.

<Callout type="warn" title="Copycats are common">
  Anyone can create a token with any name. Check the address against the real one before you trust a result. The list shows each token's price and liquidity to help.
</Callout>

## A token's page

- **Price and change**, from the pool with the most liquidity, refreshed every 30 seconds while the page is open.
- **A chart** for 1D, 7D, 30D or 1Y, from [GeckoTerminal](https://www.geckoterminal.com)'s candles for the same pool. Hover or use the arrow keys for exact values. A new token may not have history for every range.
- **Market cap, fully diluted value, liquidity and 24 hour volume.** Market cap is the data source's own estimate and can be wrong, especially for new tokens.
- **How it moved** over 5 minutes, 1 hour, 6 hours and 24 hours.
- **Where it trades** and how old its oldest pool is.
- **What you hold**, if one of your watched wallets holds it.
- **Links** to the project's website and social accounts when it has them, and to DexScreener and Solscan.
