Settings are saved in your own browser and apply at once. They are never sent to a server, so they do not follow you to another browser or device. (Your display name and picture are not settings: they are part of your [account](/docs/cotrader/account).)

## Appearance

- **Theme:** light, dark, or follow your device.
- **High contrast:** stronger text, borders and focus rings, and a plain background.
- **Reduce animations:** turns off movement across the app.

## Amounts

- **Main currency:** show amounts in SOL or in one of 50 currencies, each with its flag, found by name, code or symbol. Other currencies are worked out from the dollar price of SOL and the day's exchange rates, so they are close, not exact. If the rates cannot be read, amounts stay in SOL rather than showing a wrong number. Alert messages are written when the alert is raised and stay in SOL.
- **Second currency:** an optional small line under each amount in another currency (or SOL), marked with ≈ when it is converted. It is left out when it would repeat the main figure or when balances are hidden.
- **Hide balances:** shows dots instead of amounts, for when someone else can see your screen. Gains and losses in percent stay visible.

## Alerts in the app

- **Pop-ups:** a message in the top right corner when an alert arrives while you are in the app.
- **Sound:** a short sound with each pop-up, rising for good news and falling for bad. It needs pop-ups to be on.
- **Volume:** how loud it is. It needs pop-ups and sound to be on.

## CoTrader

- **Floating action button:** the button in the corner that opens your default CoTrader. Hover it and choose the cross to hide it, and turn it back on here. You can always chat from the Agents screen.
- **Fade pinned chats:** a chat pinned to the screen dims while you are not pointing at it or typing in it, so the page behind stays easy to read. It is on until you turn it off.
- **Fade level:** how visible a faded chat is, from 20% to 90%, and 40% to begin with. It can never be set to zero.

## Notifications

Turn on notifications on this device to get an alert when one of your rules is met, even when the app is closed. You can send yourself a test. If nothing appears, check your computer's notification settings for the browser, and that Focus or Do Not Disturb is off. On an iPhone, add the app to your Home Screen first, then open it from there.

Turning notifications on is per device and per browser. Turning them off removes that device from the list we send to.
