The **Currencies** tab on Hindsight is a currency "what if": "₦500,000 a month into dollars since 2016".

Pick the currency you **buy** and the currency you **spend**, an amount and a range. It shows what you hold, the average rate paid, the rate today, what that is worth in the currency you spent, a chart of the rate with your average marked, and a month-by-month table. Each month buys at **that month's own rate**. Today's rate is used only once, to value what you hold.

The **Download video** button makes a replay video of money put in against what it was worth, in the currency you spend.

## Where the rates come from

Rates are units of a currency per US dollar. They are official or interbank rates, not black-market ones. They are tried in this order:

| Source | Covers |
|---|---|
| US Federal Reserve H.10 on FRED | Daily rates, some back to 1971 |
| European Central Bank, through Frankfurter | About 30 currencies, daily, from 1999 |
| IMF International Financial Statistics, through DBnomics | Monthly end-of-period rates, back to the 1950s for most countries (Nigeria from 1957) |

Recent months are topped up from a daily feed. A month with no rate is left out of the totals, never guessed.

<Callout type="warn" title="Official is not street">
  In some years the official rate was far from the rate you could actually get. Replay says so in each result.
</Callout>
