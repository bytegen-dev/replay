Nothing is guessed or interpolated. If a month has no price it shows **n/a** and is left out of the totals, and the summary says how many were left out.

## Stocks and ETFs

Ordinary US-listed stocks and ETFs, with decades of history. Type a ticker or a company name: the box opens with popular stocks and searches a saved list of about 12,900 US tickers instantly, asking Yahoo only for listings abroad.

| Order | Source | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Yahoo Finance** daily chart data | Split-adjusted. Full history, and a dividend-adjusted series for **Reinvest** |
| 2 | **Tiingo**, with your own key | Full history and dividends. Optional, set in Settings |
| 3 | **Nasdaq** public data | About ten years, split-adjusted, no dividend series |

The result says which source answered and why. Each ticker's history is kept for six hours, so a repeat check doesn't ask again. If every source refuses and an older copy exists, that copy answers with a warning.

- A stock can only be bought on a day the market is open: month-end uses the last trading day on or before it, and first-of-month the first on or after.
- **Reinvest** switches to the dividend-adjusted close, which is the total return of reinvesting each payout. Dividends are ignored under Nasdaq.
- USD only. A stock quoted in another currency is refused with a clear message. Indexes (`^GSPC`) and futures aren't supported: use the ETF (`SPY`, `QQQ`).

<Callout title="Your own stock price key">
  Yahoo Finance's data is unofficial and blocks by address, so sometimes it refuses. Open **Settings** in the footer and paste a free [Tiingo](https://www.tiingo.com/) key. It stays in your browser and is sent only with stock lookups.
</Callout>

## Crypto: majors

Bitcoin, Ethereum, BNB, XRP, Solana, Cardano, Dogecoin, Litecoin, Chainlink, Avalanche and Hyperliquid (HYPE, from its own public prices, since 2024-12-05). Each has a fixed first-price date, so earlier months can't be picked.

- **Bitcoin, Ethereum and XRP** come from Bitstamp's exchange prices first (Bitcoin back to 2011), with Yahoo as the backup.
- **The others** come from Yahoo first, with Bitstamp as the backup where it lists the coin. Bitstamp's history for those is shorter, and the result says so.

## Crypto: other tokens

By address, policy id, or a name search (Ethereum, Base, BSC, Arbitrum, Solana and Cardano).

- **EVM chains and Solana:** GeckoTerminal's top pool (daily OHLCV).
- **No pool (always the case for Cardano):** CoinGecko's coin endpoints. The free tier only reaches back 365 days.
- **Cardano gaps** are filled from DexHunter (converted to USD with Bitstamp's ADA/USD close), then GeckoTerminal pools. For pool prices only, if nothing traded within 3 days, the nearest actual trade up to 7 days away is used and the Note says how far.

Search shows each token's address, price and liquidity, because copycat tokens are common. Pools under $500 of liquidity are hidden; paste the address to use one anyway.

## Choosing a day for each month

Each month targets the last day, the first day, or a chosen day, using the UTC daily point for that date. If it is missing, the nearest day within 3 days is used (ties go to the earlier) and the Note says which. Warnings flag a nearest-day price, very low volume (under $1,000 in 24 hours), and a price more than 50% away from the median of the days around it.

## The CoTrader

| For | Source | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| A watched wallet's trades, and its SOL and USDC balances | **Helius** | Public blockchain data. The first sync reads the history, later syncs only what is new |
| Current prices of the tokens a wallet holds | **DexScreener**, 30 tokens to a request | The deepest pool's price. A token with no pool holding real liquidity has no price and shows as Dead. The candle sources below are only used if DexScreener cannot be reached |
| Prices of called tokens, and price history | **Pyth**, **Birdeye**, then **GeckoTerminal** | The first that has a price is used. A token with no recent price is shown without a value |
| Token search and details | **DexScreener** | Solana pools only. The price and changes come from the pool with the most liquidity |
| Token charts | **DexScreener** | Its own embedded chart for the token's deepest pool |
| Showing amounts in another currency | **open.er-api.com** exchange rates, with SOL's dollar price | Close, not exact. If the rates cannot be read, amounts stay in SOL |
| The CoTrader's chat answers | **OpenRouter** and the model's host on the shared model, or your own provider | Sent your messages and what the CoTrader looks up for you. See [Is this safe?](/docs/cotrader/safety) |
| Email and Google sign-in | **Privy** | Creates the Solana wallet linked to a login. Its key is held by Privy, not by the CoTrader |
